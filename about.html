What is CampusReserve?

CampusReserve is the university's shared service for booking campus spaces. It brings the facilities directory, the availability schedule and booking requests together in one place, so students and staff can find a suitable room and request it without chasing paper sign-up sheets, email threads or departmental spreadsheets.

With CampusReserve you can:

  • compare facilities by location, capacity and equipment before you walk across campus;
  • check which spaces are free in a given time slot before you commit to a booking;
  • send one consistent booking request, with contact details, times and purpose included.

Facilities you can explore

Spaces are grouped into four categories. The facilities directory lists each facility's location, capacity and equipment.

Computer laboratories
Teaching laboratories with workstations, projection and wired network access, for practical sessions, programming labs and software coursework.
Lecture halls
Teaching and presentation spaces seating up to 120, with projection, amplification and hearing assistance.
Study rooms
Quiet rooms for individual or small-group revision, with a shared table, display and power sockets.
Meeting rooms
Rooms for society committees, staff meetings and advising sessions, with video conferencing for remote participants.

Who CampusReserve is for

Students
Looking for a room for a laboratory practical, a revision group or a society meeting, and needing to know it is free before they arrive.
Teaching staff
Looking for a room with a particular capacity and equipment for a class, practical or examination.
Administrative staff and facility managers
Reviewing incoming requests, approving or declining them, and keeping a single record of what has been committed to each space.

How to use CampusReserve

  1. Explore the facilities.

    Compare locations, capacities and equipment in the facilities directory.

  2. Check the schedule.

    Look up the date and time slot you need on the availability page.

  3. Send your request.

    Complete the booking request form with your contact details, the facility, the date and times, and the purpose of the booking.

  4. Follow the outcome.

    A facility manager reviews each request and approves or declines it. Reservation records are listed on the reservations page.

Please note

On this version of the site, booking requests are illustrative: submitting the form does not send, save or process a reservation, and the schedules and reservation records shown are demonstration data rather than live campus availability.
